Breast cancer

What is breast cancer?

Breast cancer is a disease which is caused when abnormal cells in the breast grow uncontrollably and form tumour. And if it spreads to other parts of the body, it becomes even more dangerous!
Breast cancer affects each ages, although the risk of having it increases substantially with increase of age. While this cancer affects both genders, around 99% of brest cancer occurs in women.

Its a global issue

Breast cancer is considered one of the world's major health challenges. An estimate of 2.4 million women were diagnosed with breast cancer and around 694,000 people died from the disease, in 2024 alone.
This risk gets worth in countries with fewer health care facilities which makes early diagnosis and access to treatment harder.

Early detection is a game changer

Detecting breast cancer early makes the treatment more effective and greatly reduces the risk of dying.

What can be done?

Reducing the harm and impact of breast cancer requires several actions:

  • Raising awareness
  • Making treatments availabile
  • Provision of reliable health cares
