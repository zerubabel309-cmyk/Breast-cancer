about us

Our mission

We are a student team working to raise awareness about breast cancer and its impact on people and communities around the world.
Our goal is to provide clear, reliable, and accessible information about breast cancer, including its symptoms, risk factors, prevention, and early detection.

Why we created this website

Breast cancer remains a major global health challenge. However, awareness and access to reliable health information vary between communities.
Through this project, we aim to help people better understand breast cancer, recognize possible warning signs, and appreciate the importance of early diagnosis and accessible healthcare.

Our Global Perspective

Breast cancer affects people worldwide, but not everyone has equal access to screening, diagnosis, and treatment. We believe that raising awareness and understanding these inequalities are important steps toward a healthier and more informed world.

Our goal

We hope this website encourages visitors to learn more, share reliable information, and understand the importance of addressing breast cancer as a global issue.
This website was created for an educational Global Perspectives project. It provides general information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
