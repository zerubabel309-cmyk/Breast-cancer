Breast cancer Prevention

Can breast cancer be prevented?

Absolutely not. There is no proven way to gurantee a person would not get breast cancer. But don't lose all hope! While breast cancer can't be fully prevented, the risk of having it can be reduced!

Having healthy livestyle such as staying active, maintaining healthy body weight, avoiding alchohol consumption and having a balanced diet help in reducing risk of developing it. Some factors contributing to breast cancers as age and inherited genetics change can't be controlled.

Early detection

While early detection doesn't prevent cancer from developing, it helps in early detection and makes effective treatments possible.
